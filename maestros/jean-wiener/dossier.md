## Career and roles

Pianist and composer. Ran the Concerts Wiéner (1921–1924) championing new music; house pianist at Le Bœuf sur le Toit from 1922; piano duo with Clément Doucet (about 1922–1938), touring and recording widely. From the 1930s, film composer for more than 300 feature and short films, plus TV and radio. Memoir *Allegro Appassionato* (1978). Most of his music is unpublished.
